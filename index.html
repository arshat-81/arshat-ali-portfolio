How I build

Things I do on every add-on, and can point to in the code.

Tested, then ported

  • Most add-ons ship with their own test suite.
  • I ported ten of them to Odoo 17 and ran all 355 tests together on a fresh database, then checked real backups by hand.
  • Every difference between Odoo versions is checked against that version’s source, not assumed.

Safe with data

  • Secrets are encrypted at rest, with a safe path from existing plaintext.
  • API keys are scoped to the models and fields an integration needs.
  • Risky actions, such as switching on an access profile or taking over a storage prefix, show a preview or ask first.

Built to survive real use

  • The migration add-on has its own job queue: row-level locking, retries with backoff and recovery of stalled jobs.
  • The object-storage add-on keeps working when the bucket is unreachable and retries later.
  • Webhooks are signed and retried until the receiver confirms.
